## Problem

You are given a sentence of lowercase English letters with words separated by one or more spaces. Write `reverseWords` to return the words in reverse order, separated by single spaces. The sentence may have leading or trailing spaces.

## Examples

- `reverseWords("learn to code")` → `"code to learn"`
- `reverseWords("  solve   problems ")` → `"problems solve"`

## Constraints

- `0 <= sentence.length <= 10000`
- The sentence contains only lowercase English letters and spaces.

## Hint

You can split the words first, then join them in reverse order.
