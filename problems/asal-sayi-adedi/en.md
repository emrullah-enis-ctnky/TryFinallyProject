## Problem

Count the positive prime numbers less than `n`. Zero and one are not prime.

## Examples

- `countPrimes(10)` → `4`
- `countPrimes(2)` → `0`

## Constraints

- 0 <= n <= 1000000.

## Hint

When a prime is found, mark its multiples as non-prime.
