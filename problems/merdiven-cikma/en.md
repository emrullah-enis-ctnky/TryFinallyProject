## Problem

You are climbing a staircase with `n` steps. Each time you can climb either 1 or 2 steps. Write a `climbStairs` method that returns the number of distinct ways to reach the top.

## Examples

- `climbStairs(2)` → `2`
- `climbStairs(3)` → `3`
- `climbStairs(5)` → `8`

## Constraints

- `1 ≤ n ≤ 45`

## Hint

You reach step `n` either from step `n-1` or from step `n-2`. Store results so you don't compute the same thing again; plain recursion is too slow for large `n`.
