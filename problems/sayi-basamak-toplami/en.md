## Problem

A digital counter needs the sum of the digits of a nonnegative integer. `sumDigits` must return that sum.

## Examples

- `sumDigits(482)` → `14`
- `sumDigits(7)` → `7`

## Constraints

- `0 <= n <= 2147483647`

## Hint

Use remainder to take the last digit and division to shorten the number.
