## Problem

Find the integer square root of nonnegative `n`, rounded down.

## Examples

- `integerSquareRoot(16)` → `4`
- `integerSquareRoot(20)` → `4`

## Constraints

- 0 <= n <= 2147483647.

## Hint

Compare the midpoint with the division result to avoid multiplication overflow.
