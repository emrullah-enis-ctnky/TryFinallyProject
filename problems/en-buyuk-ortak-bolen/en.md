## Problem

Find the greatest common divisor of two positive package sizes.

## Examples

- `greatestCommonDivisor(48, 18)` → `6`
- `greatestCommonDivisor(17, 5)` → `1`

## Constraints

- 1 <= a, b <= 2147483647.

## Hint

Apply Euclid’s algorithm by replacing the divisor with the remainder.
