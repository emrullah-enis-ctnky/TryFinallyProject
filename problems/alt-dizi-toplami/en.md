## Problem

Sum the values at even zero-based indices in a measurement array. `sumEvenIndices` must return their sum.

## Examples

- `sumEvenIndices([5, 2, 7, 1, 4])` → `16`
- `sumEvenIndices([9])` → `9`

## Constraints

- 0 <= nums.length <= 100000; -100000 <= nums[i] <= 100000

## Hint

Advance through the indices by two.
