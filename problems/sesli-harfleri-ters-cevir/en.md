## Problem

Reverse the order of vowels in a text. All other characters must remain at their original indices.

## Examples

- `reverseVowels("education")` → `"odicatuen"`
- `reverseVowels("sky")` → `"sky"`

## Constraints

- 0 <= text.length <= 100000; text contains ASCII letters.

## Hint

Move two pointers until they find vowels, then swap those vowels.
