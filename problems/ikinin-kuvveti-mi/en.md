## Problem

A memory block's size is given as `size`. Use `isPowerOfTwo` to determine whether it is a positive power of two.

## Examples

- `isPowerOfTwo(64)` → `true`
- `isPowerOfTwo(72)` → `false`

## Constraints

- `-2147483648 <= size <= 2147483647`

## Hint

The binary representation of a positive power of two has exactly one set bit.
