## Problem

Write an `addTwoNumbers` method that takes two numbers and returns their sum.

## Examples

- `addTwoNumbers(3, 5)` → `8`
- `addTwoNumbers(-2, 7)` → `5`

## Constraints

- The inputs are integers.
- Return the result instead of printing it.

## Hint

Use the `+` operator to combine the two numbers.
